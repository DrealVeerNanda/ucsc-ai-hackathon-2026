What it does

Say find my mug. It guides your hand there, one word at a time.

  1. Hands-free: the phone rides on your chest
  2. Plain words in your earbuds: right, down, forward, grab
  3. Any object you name, no setup or scanning first
  4. Private and offline: nothing leaves the GX10
Watch the hand: it moves one axis at a time and the word above it is what the wearer hears.

How it works

  1. See. The iPhone streams color and lidar depth to the GX10.
  2. Find. A detector finds the mug, a hand tracker the fingertip.
  3. Measure. Depth turns both into 3D points. Subtract them.
  4. Speak. The biggest gap becomes one word: right, down, forward, grab.
The scene does the explaining: sweep, box, the line splits into right / down / forward, then the words.
